About Me

Education

I earned my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from Texas State University. Along the way, I've built a strong foundation in areas like machine learning, software engineering, and database management. What really drives me, though, is a genuine curiosity to keep learning—especially outside the typical boundaries of computer science. That mindset has led me to explore other fields, from electrical engineering and hands-on repair work to music theory. I believe being adaptable and open to new knowledge is key to building a well-rounded skill set.

Personal Life

From a young age, I've been drawn to technology and computer science. That curiosity pushed me to teach myself the fundamentals of computer hardware and programming. By the time I was 12, I had built my first computer, and at 16, I launched my first website—mostly by diving into resources like YouTube and Google. What started as a hobby quickly became a passion, which made pursuing a degree in computer science feel like a natural next step, not just something I had to do.

Work Experience

Over the years, I've held a variety of jobs—everything from retail and mechanic work to helping out as an electrician. My first real experience with work started at age 12, pitching in with the family business. Growing up with a single mother who was building a business from the ground up meant I had to step up early on. While it wasn't always easy, I take a lot of pride in starting young—it taught me the value of hard work and built the strong work ethic I carry with me today. Now, I'm excited to turn the page and focus on the next chapter: building a successful career in computer science.
